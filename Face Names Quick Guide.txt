FACE NAMES - QUICK GUIDE

Learn your students' names and faces with flashcards built from your class rolls,
seating charts, and class photos.

Web address: https://summersbyu.github.io/face-names/

Nothing is uploaded. Students and progress stay in the browser you use, on that
device. The website holds only the app.


1. OPEN IT

Computer: open the web address in Chrome or Edge.

Phone: open it in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), then install it:
  Android: Chrome menu > Install app (or Add to Home screen > Install).
  iPhone: Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
Open it from the icon from then on. On an iPhone this matters: the icon keeps its
own data, separate from Safari.


2. TRY THE SAMPLE CLASS (optional)

On the welcome screen, click Try a sample class first: 20 made-up students to try
everything with. Remove it from the banner at the top when you're done.


3. ADD YOUR STUDENTS (on a computer)

Go to Manage > Students. Use whichever you have:

  Seating chart PDFs: click Import seating chart and pick them all. You get names,
  seats, groups, and a photo of each student.

  Class photo rolls: click Import picture roll and drag the Save picture roll
  button onto your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B shows the bar). Open a section's
  roll in Chrome and click that bookmark: it saves the roll, photos included, to
  Downloads. Repeat for each section, then Import picture roll > Choose saved
  roll files. Delete the downloaded files afterward.

  A colleague's student file: Backup > Open a file.

Each section asks for a name. Include the semester, like W27-1.


4. STUDY

Study tab, in order:
  Meet new students: a few at a time, with names, facts, and photos.
  Test yourself: recall the people you've met, on a spaced schedule.
  Practice a set: your least-known students, or a random group.
  Seating chart quiz: faces in their seats, names hidden.

In class: Open the cheat sheet shows your seating chart with names. Type a name to
find a seat; tap anyone for their photos and notes.

Before class: pick that section at the top of the Study tab to drill just them.


5. KEEP IT SAFE

Backup > Save my backup downloads everything, including your progress. Do this now
and then: clearing browser data erases the app's data.

Automatic backups (Chrome or Edge on a computer): Backup > Back up automatically >
Choose a folder. With Box Drive, your Box folder is in File Explorer under "Box"
(C:\Users\<you>\Box); make a folder there such as "Face Names backups" and choose
it. Chrome asks for permission again on each visit: click Allow and save on the
Study tab.

Phone and computer don't sync on their own. To move progress, save a backup on one
and open it on the other with Backup > Open a file > Merge.


6. SHARE WITH A COLLEAGUE

Backup > Share students with a colleague. Tick only the sections they teach, then
Save student file and send it the way you'd send other student records. They open
it with Backup > Open a file; their own progress stays theirs.


7. EACH NEW SEMESTER

Import the new sections with the semester in each name (like F27-1). The newest
semester becomes current. Earlier ones are kept for review: choose them from the
Semester menu at the top. Returning students keep their photos and progress.


IF SOMETHING'S MISSING

Manage > Needs attention lists students without a photo, class photos to finish,
sections without a seating chart, and records that may be the same student.
