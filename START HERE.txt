FACE NAMES - learn your students' names from their faces

To start:
1. Unzip this folder first (right-click the zip, then Extract All / Open With Archive Utility).
   Don't run it from inside the zip: anything you enter could be lost.
2. Move the folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents. The app's data is tied to this location.
3. Double-click index.html. It should open in Chrome or Edge. If it opens somewhere else,
   right-click it and choose Open with Chrome or Edge.

No internet connection is needed. Or skip all of this and open https://summersbyu.github.io/face-names/
in any browser, including on a phone.

Your students' names and photos stay in your browser on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
Use the Backup tab to save a copy, and keep that file private: it contains your students' photos.

If a colleague gave you a student file: open the Backup tab, click Open a file, and choose it.
It brings in the students and photos. Your own study progress starts fresh and is yours alone.

README.md has the full instructions. It opens in any text editor.
